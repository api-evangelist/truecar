Four trims built around a new turbo four, from well-equipped to near-luxury. The 2026 Jeep Grand Cherokee sticks to its two-row midsize mission, now with a turbocharged 2.0-liter four-cylinder, an eight-speed automatic, and a choice of rear- or four-wheel drive. It’s sized for drivers who want real SUV capability without moving up to a three-row.
