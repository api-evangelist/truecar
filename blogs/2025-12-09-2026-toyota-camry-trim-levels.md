The 2026 Toyota Camry has fully embraced hybrid technology. With five hybrid trims and no gas-only versions, it stands as one of the most fuel-efficient sedans on the market. All 2026 Camry models come with a 2.5-liter inline-four hybrid engine that generates 225 horsepower, paired with an automatic transmission.
